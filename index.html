PANTORY FOR iOS

Know what you have. Use it before it expires.

Pantory helps you keep track of the food in your pantry, fridge, and freezer so you can stay organized, avoid forgotten items, and waste less food.

What Pantory helps you do

  • Keep your pantry, fridge, and freezer inventory in one place
  • Scan product barcodes or add items manually
  • Track quantities, locations, categories, photos, and expiration dates
  • See expiring items in date order
  • Receive expiration reminders when notifications are enabled
  • Mark finished items and review them later in History
  • Sync your personal pantry across supported Apple devices with iCloud

Pantory Pro

  • Unlimited active pantry items
  • Share and manage a pantry with your household
  • Choose up to two expiration reminder times
  • Add finished items again from History without rescanning

Privacy

  • No Pantory account or sign-up is required
  • Your pantry data is stored using Apple technologies and can sync through iCloud
  • Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a barcode or take a photo
  • Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts to retrieve available product information
  • Privacy-friendly analytics from TelemetryDeck help improve the app
